# Official-calculator checks FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27): inputs to type

Income Tax Act 1961 as amended by the Finance Act 2025. The new-regime 87A marginal relief just above 12,00,000 is still to be confirmed: rows OC26-03 to OC26-06 test it.

Why each row: OC26-01 new TI 11,99,990: just below the 12L rebate limit (tax 0); OC26-02 new TI 12,00,000: exactly at the limit (tax 0); OC26-03 new TI 12,00,010: marginal relief, tax = the Rs 10 excess; OC26-04 new TI 12,10,000: inside the marginal-relief zone; OC26-05 new TI 12,70,580: last point where marginal relief binds; OC26-06 new TI 12,70,590: first point after the relief zone; OC26-07 new TI 16,00,010: 15% -> 20% slab; OC26-08 new TI 20,00,010: 20% -> 25% slab; OC26-09 new TI 24,00,010: 25% -> 30% slab; OC26-10 old regime unchanged: TI 5,00,010, the 5L cliff (no relief); OC26-11 new TI 50,00,010: just above the 50L surcharge threshold; OC26-12 realistic profile with HRA, 80C and 80D (old-regime deductions).

## Procedure (Advanced Calculator tab)

Setup for every row: **Advanced Calculator** tab; Act 1961; Type of ITR "Other than Updated";
AY 2026-27; Individual; Resident; leave both dates at their defaults; click the age button matching
`age_category`.

1. **New-regime run:** toggle New Tax Regime. Salaries panel, line (i) Net Salary Income =
   `adv_net_salary_new`. Enter nothing else. Record Taxable Income as `official_new_total_income`
   and Balance Tax Payable / Refundable as `official_new_tax`.
2. **Old-regime run:** toggle Old Tax Regime. Line (i) = `adv_net_salary_old`. Deductions panel:
   80C = `sec_80c`, 80CCD(1B) = `sec_80ccd1b`, 80D = `adv_80d_total`. House Property panel,
   self-occupied "Interest on Borrowed Capital" = `home_loan_interest`. Record Taxable Income as
   `official_old_total_income` and Balance Tax Payable / Refundable as `official_old_tax`.

Record "Balance Tax Payable / Refundable": it is rounded to the nearest 10 (s.288B), as our engine
reports tax. Do not record "Total Tax on Income": it is the unrounded figure and can differ by up to
Rs 5. Never record the interest (234A/B/C) or late-fee (234F) lines; they depend on the dates.
